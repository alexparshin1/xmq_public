XMQ 0.9.19 is out. It fixes what 0.9.18 broke in persistence, and makes persistent delivery twice as fast. You can download it here.

Fixed

  • A client resuming a persistent session could crash the broker when its subscription matched a retained QoS 1 or 2 message.
  • With the settings 0.9.18 shipped with, persistent delivery fell behind at 30,000 messages a second and never caught up.

60,000 persistent messages a second

On our AWS test pair, point-to-point with every session persistent: 60,000 messages a second at 210 µs, flat for the whole run. 0.9.17 managed 30,000 at 186 µs.

And with a stronger guarantee: measured with Redis syncing its log every second, so a power failure loses at most one second - earlier figures used Redis's defaults, which can lose a minute or more. See the new Persistence page.

Smaller things worth knowing

  • New persistence defaults: max_queued_writes 1000, max_redis_connections 2. Existing configurations keep their values.
  • Retained messages are sent only in answer to SUBSCRIBE, as MQTT specifies.
  • SIGHUP reloads the configuration and reopens the log instead of stopping the broker.
