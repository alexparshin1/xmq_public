Point-To-Point over TLS, five brokers on the home bench (2026-10-15)

Server thinker11 (i7-8705G, 4 cores / 8 threads, 32 GB), client thinker10 with xmq_scn --encrypted,
MQTT 5, QoS 1, 16-byte payload, ten minutes a scenario, 2 messages a second per publisher, connect
rate 10,000/s, the broker restarted before each. One self-signed certificate; TLS 1.3 (HiveMQ
negotiates AES-128-GCM, the others AES-256-GCM). tls_p2p_campaign.sh; the brokers' TLS listeners:
brokers_tls_setup.sh. Versions are the latest releases of 2026-10-15: XMQ 0.9.20 (857bd48,
SPTK f289b8a4), FlashMQ 1.27.2, Mosquitto 2.1.2, EMQX 6.3.1, HiveMQ CE 2026.5.

Median latency (RSS of the broker, peak):
Broker        50K/s              75K/s              100K/s
FlashMQ       206 us  (2.7 GB)   333 us  (4.0 GB)   343 ms  (5.3 GB)
XMQ           239 us  (1.7 GB)   756 us  (2.5 GB)   491 ms  (3.6 GB)
HiveMQ        537 us  (3.6 GB)   452 ms  (4.8 GB)   not run (above 100 ms at 75K)
EMQX          130 s   (13.2 GB)  not run            not run
Mosquitto     205 s   (1.7 GB)   not run            not run

Mosquitto, single-threaded, carried 11.8 of the 30 million messages in ten minutes. EMQX delivered
31K of the 50K a second and its queue grew for the whole run (13 GB); at the first try its TLS
listener, left at the defaults (16 acceptors, backlog 1024), lost 28 of 25,000 connections at
10,000 a second - tuned as its plain listener is (64 acceptors, backlog 8192) it connects them all.
The rate above 50K was run only for the brokers under 100 ms at the rate before.
